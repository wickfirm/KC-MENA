What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files placed on a browser or device by a website or related service. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, tags, scripts, embedded media, and server logs. They help websites operate, remember preferences, measure usage, maintain security, and support third-party features.

Cookie and usage consent

When you first visit this website, a consent notice may ask whether you agree to optional cookies and usage measurement. Essential technologies are used where needed for the website to function. Optional usage and media technologies are used only according to the choice you make where consent is required.

Categories we use

  • Essential: required for core website operation, security, consent storage, page navigation, and basic functionality.
  • Usage analytics: helps us understand how visitors use the website so we can improve content, performance, and user experience. Usage data is intended to be used in aggregated or statistical form.
  • Functional media and external services: supports embedded videos, maps, social links, font delivery, and other third-party features. These providers may process technical information under their own policies.

Third-party services

This website may link to or load resources from third-party providers, including map platforms, video or media platforms, social media, font providers, analytics tools, and group company websites. These providers may set cookies or collect technical information when you interact with their services.

How long choices are stored

Your cookie and usage consent preference is stored in your browser using local storage so the website can remember your selection. If you clear browser storage or use another device, the consent notice may appear again.

Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers allow you to block, delete, or manage cookies through settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect website performance or availability of media, maps, or other features.

Updates

This policy may be updated as website features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
